04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you show up.

Can I walk instead of run?+

Yes.

When is my place confirmed?+

After a verified payment. Direct MoMo transfers require manual verification by the organising team; submitting a transaction ID alone does not confirm your place.

What should I bring?+

Comfortable footwear, suitable clothing, your own reusable bottle if preferred, and your registration ID. Event-specific instructions will be communicated before the run.

Is the course suitable for beginners and children?+

The walk is intended to be beginner-friendly. Under-18s require guardian permission and suitable supervision. Final routes, accessibility details and safety arrangements will be communicated once approved.

Is there a medical waiver to complete?+

Yes. Please read the St. George’s Health Declaration & Participation Waiver and acknowledge it in the registration form. It is a safety acknowledgement, not medical clearance. If you have concerns about your ability to participate, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.

Do I need to share my MoMo PIN?+

No. Never provide a MoMo PIN, password or OTP to this website or an event organiser. Only authorise payments through your own wallet or the official payment provider.
