THE DOBORO EDITION

We want every participant to enjoy a welcoming and well-organised morning. Please take a moment to consider your health, the nature of the route and your responsibility to follow safety instructions.

01 / HEALTH READINESS

Know your own pace.

A 5 km walk/run involves physical exertion. Choose the distance that suits your experience and current condition. Increase or reduce your pace according to how you feel and stop if you become unwell.

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before participating if:
  • You have been advised to restrict exercise or are unsure whether an event of this distance is suitable for you.
  • You have experienced concerning symptoms during exertion, such as chest discomfort, fainting, unusual dizziness or unusual shortness of breath.
  • You have an ongoing health concern that may affect safe participation.

Do not start the event if you are acutely unwell. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis on the registration form. If you need an accommodation, contact the organising team privately before event day.

02 / THE ROUTE

Understand the environment.

Outdoor runs and walks involve foreseeable risks, including uneven ground, weather, heat, other participants and vehicles near public roads. The final approved route and briefing instructions will be communicated before the event.

Take reasonable care, follow all marshal directions and use the designated course. Participation is voluntary. This acknowledgement is not a waiver of rights that cannot legally be excluded, nor does it remove the organiser’s responsibilities to implement reasonable safety measures.

03 / WHEN HELP IS NEEDED

Consent to emergency assistance.

If urgent assistance is needed, you authorise qualified event medical personnel to provide appropriate first aid and contact ambulance or emergency services where necessary. This does not guarantee that every type of treatment will be available on the route.

Provide an accurate emergency contact when registering. The organising team will share that information only with authorised event safety personnel or emergency responders when needed.

04 / FAIR AND SAFE PARTICIPATION

Respect the experience.

  • Use your own registration and do not transfer a bib or place to someone else without organiser approval.
  • Attend the safety briefing, follow directions, respect other participants and remain within the marked course.
  • Tell a marshal if you stop participating or need assistance.
  • Participants under 18 must have their parent or legal guardian’s permission and appropriate supervision. A guardian may be required to complete the registration.
05 / PRIVACY & YOUR CHOICES

Only information we need.

We record your registration, emergency contact, payment status, and the date/version of your acknowledgement. We do not request diagnoses or medical documents through this form. Access should be limited to authorised personnel for event organisation and safety.

Permission to use identifiable photographs or videos for promotional purposes, and permission to receive future event marketing, are separate, optional choices on the registration form. Please contact the organiser if you have concerns about photography or an accessibility need.

Before public launch, the organiser must publish a complete privacy notice naming the data controller, contact address and retention period.

READY FOR THE START LINE?

Take care. Move well.

When you return to the registration form, you will be asked to confirm that you have read and understood this declaration, and to give separate consent for emergency first aid. Completing those checkboxes is not a medical assessment.
